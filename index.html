FROM THE EDITOR'S DESK

Curiosity is the brief.

I'm Aimun Malik, a BA (Hons) Media & Creative Industries graduate from the University of Warwick, with a passion for public relations, brand experiences and creative strategy. I've always believed that having an idea is only the beginning. What excites me most is turning creativity into something meaningful through thoughtful planning, bold thinking and attention to detail.

Ambitious, curious and driven, I thrive on taking initiative, embracing challenges and bringing ideas to life. My experience across marketing, branding, editorial design and events has taught me that creativity goes beyond aesthetics. It's about understanding people, recognising opportunities and creating experiences that leave a lasting impression.

Beyond my professional interests, I'm fascinated by fashion, beauty, culture and the stories behind the brands we love. I'm particularly drawn to the intersection of creativity and commerce, where imagination meets strategy. I have high expectations of myself, an appreciation for the smallest details and a genuine enthusiasm for learning. I'm proud of what I've accomplished so far, but I'm far more excited by how much there is still to achieve.

With creative curiosity,
Aimun Malik

01 / HEAD EVENTS OFFICER · WARWICK PAKISTANI SOCIETY

An entire year, brought to life.

Led the event direction for Warwick Pakistani Society across a packed year of more than 30 events, helping shape not just a calendar but a recognisable social and cultural presence. The programme included a Qawwali night, the annual PakSoc Ball and a broader series of events that strengthened visibility, attendance and community engagement across campus.

30+ EVENTS · AWARD FOR ENGAGEMENT & GROWTH
02 / DEPUTY MARKETING HEAD · WARWICK WOMEN'S CAREER

Connecting audiences and opportunities.

Supported sponsorship outreach and professional event promotion, helping secure six new sponsorships and contribute to the planning of 35 careers-focused events. Audience-led digital promotion helped drive visibility and student engagement.

6 SPONSORSHIPS · 35 EVENTS · +5,800 PROFILE VIEWS
